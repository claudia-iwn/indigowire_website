I am Claudia de Luna, a network architect and automation engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I design, automate, and modernize enterprise, data center, and campus networks. Every project starts with two questions, what problem are we solving and what are the requirements, so the result is a standard, repeatable workflow rather than a one-off.

I studied engineering at Stanford, with an emphasis in aeronautics and astronautics, and started out in software development at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. I moved into network development there, and my design of JPL's next-generation gigabit-speed backbone earned a NASA Exceptional Achievement Medal.

Since then I have worked in federal and state government, education, finance, and pharmaceuticals and biotech. For California state government, I was Senior Network Architect for a statewide network of more than 50,000 users across more than 180 sites. Several years consulting for a value-added reseller put me in front of many more clients, in banking, agriculture, manufacturing, and commercial businesses. I have run Indigo Wire Networks, my own consultancy, since 2008.

I have designed and built data centers throughout my career, including many Cisco ACI fabrics. There is less demand for ACI now that today's data centers run VXLAN and EVPN, but building those fabrics taught me how to think about abstraction in network automation. Today I build network automation in Python with Netmiko, Nornir, and Ansible, and I use Containerlab and SuzieQ to validate and verify change. For a Fortune 100 manufacturer, I was both network lead and automation lead on a segmentation program, and my automated before-and-after QA with zero-touch provisioning lets refresh projects sign off with no outstanding problems. I am a Cisco Certified DevNet Associate and a member of the DevNet 500, the first 500 people worldwide to earn a DevNet certification.

I serve on the Board of Advisors of the Network Automation Forum, gave the [opening keynote at AutoCon3](/speaking), and write about network automation at [The Gratuitous Arp](https://gratuitous-arp.net/). From 2016 to 2019 I also published ready-to-use Ansible container images on [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/u/cldeluna), each pairing an Ubuntu release with an Ansible version. The best way to reach me is through [LinkedIn](/contact).
